# Local analysis of fluorescence images

Project Leap brings astrocyte fluorescence measurement and cell counting into one desktop application for Apple Silicon Mac. Import your TIFF images, review the proposed regions or cells, and keep the results with their analysis records.

<section class="workflow-section" aria-labelledby="workflow-heading" markdown="1">
<div markdown="1">
<p class="eyebrow">WORKING WITH PROJECT LEAP</p>
<h2 id="workflow-heading">Keep the analysis and the review together.</h2>
<p>The application manages modules, analysis tasks, and saved results. Image analysis runs on your Mac, and source TIFF files stay in their original location.</p>
</div>
<ol class="workflow-steps">
<li><strong>Choose the image and channels.</strong><span>Import a supported TIFF, select the image group and time point when needed, and assign channel roles.</span></li>
<li><strong>Review the proposed result.</strong><span>Use Fiji for fluorescence regions or the built-in counting workbench for cells and their Z positions.</span></li>
<li><strong>Save and return to your work.</strong><span>Find analysis records and output files in Jobs &amp; Results. Saved counting sessions can be reopened for editing.</span></li>
</ol>
</section>
